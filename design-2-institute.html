The Coach

A background in martial arts and philosophy

Victor Pierantoni is the coach behind Liberation Coach. His background includes martial arts and a long interest in how Zen and Stoic philosophy relate to daily life. He hosts the Zen Stoic Path Show — more than 180 conversations — and coaches online from Austin, Texas.

The Method

Reflection and emotional awareness

You and Victor work through reflective questions about a specific situation — what happened, what you are assuming it means, and what you would need to find out before deciding. Part of the work is noticing the emotional response underneath, so you can choose a next step instead of reacting to it.

One situation at a time. Each conversation starts from what you bring, then reviews what you tried and what you learned.

  1. What happened?

  2. What are you assuming it means?

  3. What do you need to find out before deciding?

Questions

What to know before you apply

Are sessions online?

Yes. Coaching is delivered online by Victor, so you can work together from wherever you are.

Who is it for?

Founders, business owners and adult athletes aged 18 and older who want to work on how they respond to a specific challenge.

What happens after I apply?

Applying opens a no-obligation discovery Zoom call with Victor — a conversation to see if the work fits, not a booked session and not automatic acceptance.

What is Circle access?

All coaching members receive access to Circle alongside their 1:1 work; your coach will walk through what that includes when you begin.

Is coaching the same as therapy?

No. Performance coaching is focused on a present situation and the next step you want to take; it isn't therapy or medical treatment.
